# P3.2 runtime closure contract review

Date: 2026-10-02. Report-only review of `next-runtime-closure-contract.md` (145 lines as reviewed), its brief, P3.1 admission contract, adopted P2 contract, bounded relevant source, and primary mechanism documentation. Only this review file is owned by this reviewer.

**Verdict: accept the confinement architecture in principle; require the three Important corrections below before adopting this as an implementation-ready end-to-end contract.** None requires a general container framework, new privilege broker, hardware attestation, or a different P2 predicate. No host availability or positive execution was tested.

The proposed owner-provisioned supervisor domain, private byte-copy capture, pinned non-setuid bubblewrap, immutable child runtime view and pre-provisioned bounded noexec work filesystem are a reasonable narrow choice under the stated trusted owner/kernel/supervisor/verifier boundary. The document correctly declines to protect against a malicious admitted interpreter or owner. A runtime manifest alone would be insufficient; here the intended authority is the trusted collector plus actual confinement and real selected verification.

## Important 1 — name and specify the new current-admission consumer

Locations: `next-runtime-closure-contract.md:109-113,127`; `next-current-admission-contract.md:48-56,66-75`.

The future fixture says that current admission authenticates the precise P2 execution tuple. Existing P3.1 explicitly admits only `parity-check` v1 / `parity-binding-v1`, requires `execution_profiles=[]`, validates exact legacy RESULT fields, and reports runtime closure unevaluated. Its source implements those restrictions at `src/agentdecompile_recovery/corpus/current_admission.py:264,325-327,416-425,434-451`. P2 uses `parity-region-check` v1, six inputs, three outputs and `parity-region-coff-i386-leaf-v1` (`next-parity-region-contract.md:136-166`). An optional RESULT closure field is not accepted by the existing exact-field reader.

Concrete failure: a real confined P2 verification is collected and independently signed exactly as proposed, but the P3.1 loader refuses its nonempty execution profile before reading it; retaining an empty profile only moves refusal to the P2 task/output/method checks. Relaxing those checks silently would change P3.1's admitted predicate and still supply no runtime-profile validation.

Required correction:

- Explicitly keep `current-signed-observation-v1` and its schema unchanged. Name a separate future versioned qualified-P2 admission schema and read API, for example `agentdecompile.current-native-region-admission.v1` and `authenticate_native_region_execution(cfg, selection)`.
- Specify its exact owner-pinned runtime profile entry: kind/version, emitted method, P2 checker/comparison policy, root/source/launcher/filter/environment/entrypoint pins, allowed signer roles/keys, and coherent admission generation. Reuse existing public-only signature/lineage validation, but do not treat the P3.1 observation entry as that authority.
- Give exact closure, RESULT extension and enclosing VERIFIED evidence schemas, field types, allowed fields, caps and refusal semantics. State which original task/result/proposal digests must agree with the signed records and which refs are diagnostics. Define separate `verifier_execution_qualified` and `producer_execution_qualified` conclusions: reverifying an old proposal cannot qualify its producer.
- Make the hash graph acyclic explicitly: canonical child verification `V0` and exported object refs -> supervisor closure `C` -> enclosing evidence `{lane_evidence, execution_closure_ref:C}` -> signed VERIFIED. `C` must not contain its own digest or the digest of that enclosing evidence. A process-local `ExecutionBinding` may retain the later evidence digest without inserting it into `C`. Producer collection similarly signs `{result_ref, execution_closure_ref}` outside the result bytes.
- Amend fixture completion: execution/collection can succeed first; **current admission of that tuple remains unavailable until this separately versioned reader is implemented and genuinely owner-configured**. This reader still does not supply compile closure, source/TU joins, corpus acceptance or whole-image acceptance.

This is the controller's named compatibility risk, confirmed in both contract and source. It is an actual absent consumer, not an enterprise policy enhancement.

## Important 2 — bind current policy at the actual deferred signing boundary

Locations: `next-runtime-closure-contract.md:111-113`; ownership list at `:31-35`.

The existing `_collect` methods do not sign immediately. They queue a `PendingRecord`: verifier collection at `src/agentdecompile_grid/verify/loop.py:239-246`, producer collection at `src/agentdecompile_grid/worker/loop.py:599-603`. `worker/outbox.py:131-140` persists that unsigned body; `:181-245` exports then calls `writer.write_batch` on later flush/retry. `transport/local.py:154-174` actually signs during that call; the other appender seals during `log/outbox.py:118-121`.

Concrete failure: capture/verification/collection succeed under admission A; the unsigned pending record is persisted; A is revoked or its profile pin changes during transport backoff or a supervisor restart; ordinary outbox replay signs and publishes the previously queued qualified positive under the new current state. A check only in `_collect` does not implement the contract's promised refusal of publication after an admission change. Existing outbox entries do not carry a trusted live launch handle, and ordinary pending JSON must not reconstruct one.

Required correction:

- Distinguish launch admission, completed collection admission and signing/publication admission. Specify a narrowly scoped qualified record writer/guard at `write_batch` that reloads effective owner authority immediately before each seal attempt, authenticates the exact persisted closure/export tuple, and requires the same admitted generation/pins and eligible independent keys. Unqualified writes retain current behavior.
- State crash semantics. The smallest first profile may quarantine/drop **unsigned qualified** pending events after supervisor restart and require a fresh execution; it must not re-create a live capability from queue JSON. If durable qualified replay is required instead, explicitly define the trusted durable collector record and revalidation protocol. Do not accidentally implement both.
- Identify ownership for `worker/outbox.py` and the selected signing writer boundary, or name a concrete wrapper installed there. The present file list omits the place that can enforce this requirement.
- Publish/export all exact transitive evidence refs before the record. An existing-object check or outbox `_confirmed` cache is not fresh content authentication of foreign/mutable CAS bytes. Collection's bounded hash-and-reread requirement remains mandatory; specify the supported trusted local immutable-CAS durability premise for delayed publication rather than quietly expanding this slice to arbitrary transports.
- Define the linearization point as the coherent owner-policy read used for the actual seal. Later revocation is handled by the new current reader; no impossible promise of an atomic transaction with arbitrary external policy-file replacement is needed.

Retain the simple one-use in-memory launch handle for live collection. It is useful authority within the trusted supervisor process; class constructor visibility alone is not a security boundary against arbitrary host Python, which is already outside this profile's attacker model.

## Important 3 — close the concrete admission/dispatch/staging boundary before reusing the loop

Locations: `next-runtime-closure-contract.md:31-37,91-107,117-121`.

The contract requires bounded authenticated proposal reads and a total ceiling beginning before staging. Yet the existing `VerifierLoop._make_job` performs `self.objects.get` or an upstream fallback and permissive decoding **before** `sandbox.launch` (`src/agentdecompile_grid/verify/loop.py:190-201`). `StepMachine` invokes `_make_job` before obtaining `RunningJob.started_at` (`worker/loop.py:441-459`). Thus merely adding the sandbox and binding to `JobOutcome` leaves an unbounded/remote proposal read outside the proposed timer and local-only input boundary.

There are two further exact interface gaps: the generic grid backend is described as recovery-agnostic but `stage_job_inputs` depends on an unspecified recovery ref extractor; and “narrowly enumerated” prerequisite operations are not actually enumerated. P1/target are SMALL tasks while the proposed capture/staging/operation reservation uses the P2 MEDIUM ceiling. A SMALL proposal producer cannot simply inherit the stated 600-second contract without an explicit operation budget rule.

Concrete failure: select an oversized or remote-only proposal, or an input graph missing a P2 proposed inventory. Existing job construction blocks/loads it before qualified admission, or the actual `ParityRegionCheck.verify` fails when it reads its three proposal outputs. A generic recursive ref walker or host fallback introduced to repair that miss would cross the intended boundary.

Required correction:

- Make the first supported operation exactly `verify` / `parity-region-check` / 1 / dispatch `kind`, with the fixed P2 params and native checker identity. Existing authentic prerequisite proposals may be consumed; required missing proposals remain a separately authorized prerequisite. Defer qualified producer RESULT and standalone P1/target operations to a subsequent explicit table, or enumerate their exact schemas/budgets now. The former is the smaller first slice.
- Introduce the qualified branch **before** the existing `_make_job` read. It retains immutable locators, establishes the admission timestamp/deadline, then performs bounded no-follow authenticated local reads; no ordinary `get` or upstream fallback is on this branch. Keep the ordinary branch unchanged.
- Place fixed P2 schema selection in a recovery-owned module. It yields an immutable data-only staging plan containing exact role/ref/cap tuples to the generic backend. It cannot supply callables, imports, paths, store locators, policies or arbitrary recursive JSON traversal. The grid layer only copies/authenticates this fixed plan; no generic plugin protocol is necessary.
- Enumerate the role table, including the selected P2 proposal's `receipt`, `target_inventory`, `candidate_inventory`; six P2 inputs; target/P1 task and result envelopes; target snapshot/image/selection/implementation and proposal output refs; P1 receipt/raw refs; and the exact nested verifier evidence refs exported after recomputation. Repeated refs deduplicate under the smallest applicable role cap. Define unknown/missing/extra fields as refusal rather than fetching them dynamically.
- Spell out entrypoint normalization. The real P2 method returns `verifier_key=""` (`parity_region.py:577-578`); existing `run_manifest` fills the admitted key (`worker/sandbox.py:404-413`). The measured fixed shim must perform that one authorized assignment after checking task/result/method, then the parent compares all fields before accepting them. Do not reject every real lane result because its internal unspecialized key is empty, and do not reuse the host manifest's arbitrary-import/store branch.
- Define the finite `RunningJob` stages: capture -> stage -> launch/barrier -> run -> tree termination -> export -> collected/quarantined. `poll()` must remain bounded; `exited` must mean owned tree gone and bounded export complete, because existing `_reap` removes the slot and immediately calls collection/cleanup (`worker/loop.py:343-356`). Specify phase-deadline enforcement internally, not only the outer 600-second check, and prevent filesystem helper I/O from surviving cleanup and later mutating a quarantined allocation.

This correction belongs in the qualified branch and fixed recovery adapter. It does not justify rewriting the worker scheduler, adding arbitrary admission callbacks or changing P2's deterministic predicate.

## Mechanism assessment and implementation constraints

| Concern | Assessment under this trust boundary |
|---|---|
| Other writers and donor replacement | Private distinct capture inodes plus the owner pin and trusted supervisor's no-write lifetime promise close the stated donor mutation race. A read-only bind of the mutable donor would not. Rejecting unrelated unconfined processes under the supervisor UID is a necessary explicit deployment premise; do not pretend `0700` establishes it. |
| Source/import closure | Complete captured inventory, fixed entry shim/path, `-I -S -B`, no bytecode caches and no writable import/config path are coherent for trusted fixed code consuming data. Target helper reconciliation remains mandatory. The actual package initializer imports other lanes (`grid_lanes/__init__.py:34`), and target's fixed helper closure is substantially larger than three files (`target.py:639-665`); the inventory must include those transitive imports. Avoid a new lazy-import refactor just to make this fixture smaller. |
| ELF/loader closure | Complete immutable filesystem plus no host mounts/FD aliases and fixed loader environment is a sensible allowed-loading-universe claim. Pin recursive interpreter/native module/SQLite/checker loader dependencies, reject executable stacks and writable/relative search paths, and fail unsupported artifacts rather than weakening the filter. This does not prove every included library was loaded or qualify memory safety. |
| Noexec work | Requiring an actual bounded owner-provisioned noexec filesystem is narrower than introducing a mount broker. It blocks native execution/mapping from scratch, not Python interpretation. The fixed admitted code must not use task data as `eval`/`exec`/pickle/import/config; that trust is explicitly stated. No claim of hostile-interpreter isolation is needed. |
| Nested P1 process operations | The proposed filter deliberately permits ordinary clone/fork, setsid, wait and signal operations. Those are actually required: P1 uses `Popen(start_new_session=True)`, `waitid(WNOWAIT)`, pipe/select I/O and `killpg` (`parity.py:425-501`). No source-level contradiction was found in that path. Actual pinned CPython/libc/checker compatibility remains a later real fixture obligation, especially executable mapping policy. Do not “learn” and widen the filter from a passing sample. |
| IPC and owned identities | Per-message kernel credentials, fixed private inherited descriptors and a nonce are appropriate; a pre-fork `SO_PEERCRED` is insufficient. Choose the socketpair protocol for v1 rather than leaving pipes as an undefined equivalent. Require pidfds for both namespace init and entrypoint for this first profile unless a specific equally anchored fallback is specified. Include frame sizes, stages, credential/PID association and close/reject behavior in the implementation contract. |
| Startup and process lifetime | The launcher-reported child PID is the namespace init, not automatically the final Python process. Reconcile that trusted launcher status with the actual entrypoint sender before releasing task bytes. Establish the exact startup checks that remain possible under the nonprivileged supervisor; unavailable `/proc` identity checks must fail closed, never become child self-reports. Keeping the namespace init closes detached `setsid` descendants; a monitor exit alone does not. |
| Resource costs | Copying up to 1 GiB, staging up to 512 MiB, and maintaining root/module/export inventories is meaningful work. Phase caps may refuse larger but otherwise valid artifacts; they are not performance guarantees. A 1-GiB work allocation bound requires a real filesystem/quota premise, not merely checking `noexec`. Provisioning remains outside source implementation. |

Primary documentation checked for these bounded points: [bubblewrap v0.11.0 source](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/containers/bubblewrap/v0.11.0/bubblewrap.c), [Python 3.12 command-line behavior](https://docs.python.org/3.12/using/cmdline.html), [UNIX socket credential semantics](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man7/unix.7.html), and access rules for [process executable](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man5/proc_pid_exe.5.html) / [process root](https://man7.org/linux/man-pages/man5/proc_pid_root.5.html). Use the selected Python 3.12 documentation in the contract instead of its current 3.14 link. These are mechanism/source findings, not observations of this machine.

The pinned launcher source supports the intended separation: it reports its namespace child before launch, forks an init and final program, closes extra descriptors in init, and applies the supplied filter before program exec. Therefore status/sync/control channels must have separately defined roles; `--block-fd` release alone must not be mistaken for the Python-ready/filter-validated barrier. Exact FD allocation and handshake are implementation requirements, not a reason to invent a new launcher.

## Recommended first implementation slice

After the corrections are adopted, implement **one qualified P2 verifier execution**, with no catalog/provider wiring or provisioning:

1. Recovery ownership: a fixed `NativeRegionVerifyPlan` builder with the exact task/proposal/data-ref schemas and limits, plus the measured fixed entry shim calling real `ParityRegionCheck.verify`. It supplies data plans to the grid backend, not callbacks.
2. Grid sandbox ownership: fixed profile validation, private capture, confinement, startup socket protocol, pidfd-owned namespace lifecycle, and bounded export. `launch(plan) -> RunningJob`; `collect()` yields a one-use supervisor-issued `CollectedExecution` only after tree termination/export.
3. Grid collection ownership: compare the slot/task/result/method/key before replacement, construct the acyclic closure/evidence DAG and queue all authenticated refs. Failures are NOTE/unverifiable; no producer penalty for infrastructure failure.
4. Signing ownership: a qualified-only current-policy seal guard with explicit restart behavior, installed at the actual outbox writer. Do not infer authority from a serialized `ExecutionBinding`.
5. Separate reader ownership: implement the newly named versioned qualified-P2 reader. Until that slice and owner provisioning exist, report confined execution/collection evidence only; never describe P3.1 as admitting it.

Qualification later uses the contract's real official objdiff/`31 c0 c3` fixture and genuine independent signer lineage. Required negatives include delayed unsigned publication after revocation/restart, oversized proposal before launch, missing P2 inventory export, foreign response PID/nonce, detached checker, donor mutation, writable import/module attempts and cleanup failure. These are specifications only; none were executed.
