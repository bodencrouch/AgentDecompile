## Complete contract checks

**Creation and continuity:** the sole birth producer performs exclusive fresh-namespace bootstrap, full bounded canonical log validation and durable issuance before initialization. Tombstones are not removed or rewritten. Confirmed acquisition contention is a no-effect wait; later write/fsync errors retain the namespace fault. Byte/record/issued-ID limits block new issuance without poisoning healthy existing witnesses. Existing-ID absence, corrupt ledgers, historical JSON and lost authority sessions cannot initialize replacement budgets. A healthy witness is created only after successful row durability and preserves the complete snapshot, both ledgers, attempts and allocation history.

**Mutation and ordering:** `_write` requires the canonical object, captured authority and exact durable snapshot; it advances the committed revision only after the atomic replacement and directory sync complete. The uncertain candidate remains distinct on failure. Registry checks use object identity; release and capacity retirement take expected objects. Gate-before-registry ordering is retained, and global preparation locking does not enclose job admission or worker waits. Workflow receives detached snapshots and returns detached callback results; named basis-checked edits cover knowledge, fingerprint, family membership, stages, progress, target and toolchain state. No full workflow/child wait is wrapped in the authority gate.

**Publication and frozen inputs:** comparison artifacts use generation/receipt-specific paths and become reusable only through a successful guarded row-reference commit (`preparation.py:2938–2994`). An orphan artifact is not selected by filename guessing. Captured receipt paths require detached inventory and do not execute the optional dynamic DB fallback. The controller-provided `task-9-matcher-projection.diff` shows exactly the optional keyword-only DB parameter and connection-path selection changed in `_matching_input_digests`; matcher-input-v2 payload, selector, columns and ordering are otherwise unchanged.

**Handoff, capacity and jobs:** root intent is committed before `STORE.start`; allocated ID and start-attempt observation are private and precede thread start. Definite pre-attempt failure retires only its token; attempted-start uncertainty prevents replacement and retains capacity until exact completion/non-entry disposition. The outer `JobStore._run` finally covers execute prebody refusal and early `_run_body` returns. Finished faulted coordinators release execution capacity separately from retained fault leases. Stale finalizers and list observation cannot retire a successor. Three child admission branches and three cancellation calls use the captured gate/accepted child identity; no new permission to kill or release accepted child resources is introduced.

**Commands, surfaces and security:** commands use a separate short OS lock, coherent read/merge, monotonically increasing revision and durable acknowledgement; the lock ends before resume/admission. No ordinary command or restored I/O clears a fault or refunds spending. All declared adapters use the bounded shared body and distinguish 409/503 from validation 400 and successful 202. Open/register preserves enclosing success with a nested preparation decision. Capabilities stay on private live records or closures; copies, history, public serialization and direct request context do not grant them. The private child execution context is consumed for defaults/root and is not serialized or confused with root lifecycle authority. Ordinary calls with no private context retain their existing defaults and job path.

**Regressions:** final test source uses a genuine fresh namespace, sole birth producer, real future OS leases, both initialized ledgers, guarded writes and exact execution slots. It does not bypass `_write` to bless an injected authority. The authored cases preserve raw durable bytes, first-fault causes, start counts/IDs, accounting history and unaffected controls. They cover S1–S7, malformed queue/inventory/recovery rows, real JobStore prebody and early persistence-return finalizers, ordinary-job success, all five refusal kinds at adapters, child frozen defaults/root and conflicting DB refusal, and raw path-spelling identity.
