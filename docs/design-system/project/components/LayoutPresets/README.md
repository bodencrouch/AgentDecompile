# LayoutPresets

Saved task layouts — Read, Compare, Review, Triage, Verify rebuild, Explain build difference and Walk pointers — each defining which panes are open, their sizes, which panes follow, derive or hold, and the dock tab, switched with g then 1…7 without touching the selection. Keys 6 and 7 are proposed: `keymap.json` registers `layout.preset` as g then 1…5 until they are integrated.

**Use it** from the NavigationBar's **Layout ▾** menu button, the View menu, the palette, and `layout.preset` in `keymap.json` (g then 1…5 inside any list, grid or tree; g then 6 and g then 7 proposed). A preset is a way to look, not a way to work: it rearranges panes and nothing else.

**Consumer provides** the seven preset definitions (open panes in each region, annotation layers, center split and which pane is primary, following, derived or held slot per pane, navigator, evidence and dock sizes and collapsed state, dock tab) with the viewer's saved changes as a diff; the active preset and whether the current layout differs from it; the current splitter values; and the surface's capabilities (the static 8080 surface has no split panes).

- **Five presets**, in keymap order:
  1. **Read** (g 1) — Functions in the navigator; Listing and Decompile side by side, both following; the evidence rail on References and "Why this name"; dock collapsed.
  2. **Compare** (g 2) — Functions; SplitCompare with the primary listing following and a derived counterpart pane, under the CounterpartStrip in its expanded form; evidence rail at its default width; dock collapsed.
  3. **Review** (g 3) — the review queue in the navigator; MatchReview master-detail (ReviewDetail) with a held endpoint pair beside it; CandidateAlignment in the evidence rail; dock on Problems.
  4. **Triage** (g 4) — a PipelineMap band across the top of the center; FailureRecord and ObjectiveSummary in the evidence rail; the ProblemsList dock expanded. It does not set the navigator or the view under the band; both stay as they were.
  5. **Verify rebuild** (g 5) — navigator collapsed; SourceRepairDiff and ObjectDiff side by side, both following; ImageDeltaMap below them; VariantLedger in the evidence rail; dock on Jobs.
  6. **Explain build difference** (g 6, proposed) — Functions in the navigator; the CounterpartStrip in its expanded form above a SplitCompare of the primary listing, following, and a derived counterpart pane, with the Compiler layer on in both panes (`layers=compiler`); VariantRelation for the current pair in the evidence rail (below); ToolchainEvidence available but not opened; dock collapsed.
  7. **Walk pointers** (g 7, proposed) — Functions in the navigator; DecompileView and PointerWalk side by side, both following; TypeEditor, read-only, in the evidence rail showing the type of the walk's current step (below); dock collapsed.
- **Explain build difference, in detail.** The derived pane is the selection's counterpart on the build the CounterpartStrip marks, and the pair becomes `cmp`; with no recorded counterpart the pane reads "No counterpart of ‹entity› on ‹build›" and nothing is derived. The Compiler layer shows each side's gutter tags and the cross-build note between rows; a build without detector output reads "No detector output for ‹build› · Not loaded". The rail carries VariantRelation's row for the current `cmp` pair, scoped to the selection ("For ResourceRef::Resolve · build-a ↔ build-c"): the entity's variance class with its evidence level and deciding evidence, each tool's opinion, and the pair's toolchain, flag and link differences; **Show all** opens `ws=compare&panel=variants`. ToolchainEvidence is one Go away, from the rail's toolchain values or pair link (`ws=evidence&panel=toolchain&build=…`); the preset never opens it, so no expensive corpus view loads because of a layout. Every class keeps its level word: a class is solid only when source, toolchain and flag identity are recorded for both builds. VariantRelation documents its rail form (one pair, scoped to the selection).
- **Walk pointers, in detail.** PointerWalk follows the code cursor after the settle and publishes `walk`; the preset does not open a walk by itself, so until `p` is pressed on an expression the pane reads "Selection is not an address expression; nothing to walk". The rail's TypeEditor is read-only here: it follows the walk's current step (unlike an open editor, which follows nothing), shows the component grid with one offset column per build, and has no draft, Save or Propose. **Edit on ‹build›…** opens a real TypeEditor bound to one explicit build, in the center, with its own confirm tiers. Neither pane decompiles, re-types or analyzes to draw itself ("Not decompiled in this session · Enter to decompile"). TypeEditor documents this read-only following form.
- Sizes not named by a preset use the size tokens' defaults (`navigator-w`, `evidence-w`, `dock-h`); "expanded" and "collapsed" are the region's own states, restored by Enter on its splitter.
- **Switching keeps the selection.** Project, logical entity, inspected build, address, the checked set, marks, the active worklist, drafts, an open walk and held panes are untouched. The status line says "Layout: Verify rebuild. Selection unchanged … Project unchanged. No work started." Focus stays where it was (g 1…7 is pressed in a list, and that list keeps focus) or moves to the same kind of pane; it is never dropped to the page body.
- **Never changes the project or starts work.** A preset has no action, no admission and no project field. A pane that needs unloaded evidence shows "Not loaded. Open to read it."; nothing is fetched or analyzed because a preset opened it.
- **Following, derived and held stay distinct.** Following panes are a function of the one selection (1px `line` frame, "Follows selection"). A derived pane, like Compare's counterpart pane, follows through the selection's counterpart and says "Counterpart of #88 · derived" (dotted frame in the schematic). A held slot, like Review's endpoint pair, is reserved but never filled by the preset: it reads "Held pair · empty until you hold" in a dashed 2px `ink-strong` frame and fills only when the reviewer presses o (`review.open`) or Open beside on an endpoint. Existing held panes stay held across a switch and keep their target build; if the new preset has no room, they become kept evidence cards, never closed silently. At most two center panes, as the linked-views rules require; Review's pair is one held SplitCompare.
- **Triage's rail shows what you open.** FailureRecord and ObjectiveSummary in the rail show the problem row opened with Enter in the dock, never the row merely focused (WCAG 3.2.1).
- **Per viewer, durable.** Presets and the viewer's changes live in durable workspace state as a diff against the defaults. A changed layout shows "modified" beside the preset name (a dashed-frame word, never color); the menu offers **Save changes to ‹preset›** with what changed ("evidence 260 → 360") and **Reset ‹preset› to default**.
- **Location.** Presets and sizes are never in the URL. A switch pushes one history stop only when it changes the workspace (`ws`); otherwise it replaces `views`, `cmp`, `layers` and `dock` in place, so Back does not step through layouts.
- **Splitters restore accessibly.** Every splitter is a focusable `role="separator"` whose `aria-valuenow`, `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax` and `aria-valuetext` ("360 pixels, changed from 260") are restored with the preset; bounds come from the size tokens (`navigator-min`…`rail-max`, `evidence-min`…`rail-max`, `dock-min`…`dock-max`, max 46dvh). A preset writes `--navigator-w`, `--evidence-w` and `--dock-h` from durable layout state. The pane-header size menu and the View menu set the same sizes without dragging (WCAG 2.5.7).
- The menu uses `menuitemradio` rows with "g 1"…"g 7" in mono; the preset gallery (the View menu's **Panes and sizes…**) draws each preset as a schematic of its regions in `pane`, `well` and `raised` boxes framed in `line-control`, labelled in words, with the definition as a description list beside it. The current preset takes the selection shape and the word "current".
- **Surfaces.** Both read the same definitions. On the static 8080 surface there are no split panes: the counterpart pane and the held endpoint pair open as a new window at the same link ("Open beside (new window)"), Walk pointers shows PointerWalk as a rail section above the read-only TypeEditor, and rails, sizes, layers and the dock tab still apply (◐ partial, said once). The 8080 review window uses ReviewDetail's in-place form with the same fields. Below 700px a preset chooses the starting pane and dock tab only (Explain build difference starts on the split tablist's primary segment, Walk pointers on PointerWalk); NarrowFlow shows one pane at a time.

**Linking**

- **Publishes:** nothing on its own. A switch may change `ws` (one history stop, only when the workspace changes) and replaces `views`, `cmp`, `layers` and `dock` in place; it never changes `project`, `entity`, `build`, `addr`, `hold`, `keep` or `wl`. Presets and sizes are never in the URL.
- **Follows:** the one selection, through the following and derived panes it opens (Walk pointers' read-only TypeEditor follows the walk's current step). A held slot is filled only by `o` or Open beside. Not holdable.
- **Targets rendered:** no entity links; the status line after a switch names the unchanged selection as text.
- **Peek source:** none.
- **Absent copy:** a pane the surface cannot show is named with what happens instead ("opens as a new window"); a region a preset leaves alone says "as it was"; an empty held slot says "Held pair · empty until you hold"; a pane the preset leaves unopened on purpose says how to open it ("ToolchainEvidence · Go from the pair row"; "Press p on an expression to walk it").
- **Target-size exception:** splitter hit zones are 6px (`splitter-hit`) and rely on the WCAG 2.5.8 Equivalent exception: the splitter keys, the pane-header size menu and the View menu reach every size. Menu rows and cards are 24px or larger; 44px below 700px and with a coarse pointer.

**Keyboard**

- `layout.preset`: g then 1…5 while focus is in a list, grid or tree (composite scope, never in text fields or edit mode); the which-key hint after g lists them. g then 6 (Explain build difference) and g then 7 (Walk pointers) are proposed: no leader letter or digit in guideline 85 uses 6 or 7, and `keymap.json` gains them only at integration. Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+digit is not used because it types characters on AltGr keyboards. Remappable in KeymapSettings.
- From anywhere else: the NavigationBar's **Layout ▾** (an APG Menu Button: Enter, Space or ↓ opens; ↑/↓ move; Enter or Space picks), the View menu or the palette. Esc follows the one Esc order in the Keymap section.
- Splitters (APG Window Splitter): arrows 16px (`splitter-step`), Shift+arrows 64px, Home and End go to the minimum and maximum, Enter collapses and restores. Reset is **Reset** in the pane-header size menu, or **Reset ‹preset› to default** in the Layout menu.

**Accessibility**

- A switch is announced once in the polite region: "Layout: Verify rebuild. Selection unchanged." Nothing else is announced.
- `aria-keyshortcuts` cannot express a two-key sequence, so menu items carry "g then 1…7 in any list, grid or tree" as their description and show "g 1" as text.
- Presets change layout only on explicit activation, never on focus or hover (WCAG 3.2.1); the selection and project never change (3.2.2).
- Schematics are `role="img"` with a text alternative that lists panes, following, derived and held slots and the dock state; the description list beside each says the same in words.
- Held, derived, modified and current are words and frames, not color. Targets are 24px, 44px below 700px and with a coarse pointer (2.5.8).

**Removes switching:** presets claim no journey of their own; they save the arrangement that other units' journeys use.
