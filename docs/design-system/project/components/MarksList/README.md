# MarksList

Per-viewer marks on any address or entity, the program's own bookmarks (read-only), and worklists collected from any result set and stepped with F8 and Shift+F8 without leaving the current view. It covers marks, worklists and the stepper; there are no separate Marks, Worklist or Bookmarks components.

**Use it** as a navigator section ("Marks and worklists"), as the target of `g '` and of the `'` scope in go to anything, and as the worklist stepper while a worklist is active. It replaces hand-kept lists of addresses and the habit of returning to a result list after every item.

**Consumer provides** per mark: its lowercase letter or its name, the target (a concrete build and address, or for a logical mark the entity across builds), an optional note, the basis it was made against and whether that basis changed, and whether the target still resolves. Per program bookmark, read through the existing bookmark operation: build, category, comment and address. Per worklist: id, name, the source it was collected from (review queue page and match-id cursor, Problems filter, reference results, readiness cells or any other result set) with its link and snapshot time, the ordered items with their targets, which items were visited and which the viewer marked handled, the current position, and whether an item was decided elsewhere. Marks and worklists come from durable workspace state for this viewer.

- **Quick marks are lowercase a–z, per viewer** (`list.mark`: m then a letter; `list.jumpMark`: ' then a letter), in lists, grids and code wells. A letter mark is scoped to the concrete build and address where it was set. The letter is a 20px `ink-strong` framed mono glyph with the accessible name "Mark a". Setting a letter that is taken moves it and says so in the polite region ("Mark a moved from FUN_0041c880 to ResourceRef::Resolve").
- **Named and logical marks go through the palette** ("Set mark…", "Set logical mark…", also in the row menu). A logical mark names the entity and resolves on other builds through the locator, saying so when a build has no counterpart. Jumping to a mark on another build is an explicit build switch, stated first.
- **Marks survive reload and basis changes.** They live in durable workspace state, never in the URL. A mark whose basis changed shows ◷ `fact-stale` "basis changed since marked". One whose target vanished reads "no longer resolves on build-d · mark kept, nothing guessed" and is not a link; it is never called absent.
- **Notes are view state, not evidence.** A mark's note is labelled with the word "note" and has no claim-tier frame; marks, notes and worklists carry no claim tier. Mark notes are per-viewer view state. They are not a context piece (F14) and are not read by context fusion, propagation, agent context bundles (F21) or exports, unless the operator adds them through the catalog's add-context action.
- **Program bookmarks are not marks.** A read-only group "Program bookmarks · ‹build›" lists the analysis program's own bookmarks: category, comment and the address as an address link (Enter goes). They are read through the existing bookmark operation with no new backend, and are the same entries as the Bookmarks navigator scope and the `'` go-to scope. "Save as program bookmark" arms the catalog's bookmark action for one build; the bookmark appears after a re-read.
- **Worklists come only from Collect.** `w` (`list.worklist`) on any focused result set, **Collect into worklist…** in its toolbar, a run summary chip ("Collect failed as worklist") or a FilterChip's menu opens a small dialog: name, source link, snapshot time, and the sentence "It changes nothing in the project and starts no work." ProblemsList's **Step problems** does the same in one activation. A run or a refusal never becomes a worklist by itself. A worklist is a snapshot of that result set in its order; new rows at the source are reached through the source link, never merged in silently.
- **One active worklist** at a time, carried as `wl=`. Its row shows the selection shape and an **Active · F8** word chip with a 2px `ink-strong` frame. The active worklist is never replaced silently. Another viewer's `wl=` reads "Worklist not available here".
- **Progress words.** "position 4 of 12 · 7 visited · 3 handled", never a percentage. **Visited** is automatic: the item was the primary selection. **Handled** is an optional per-viewer toggle, described "your checklist · not a review decision · starts no work". Never "done", "complete", "reviewed" or "checked off"; visited ≠ reviewed. A Problems worklist reads "Problems · 2 of 5 · snapshot 14:02".
- **One presentation per width.**
  - At 700px and wider the stepper is a toolbar under the NavigationBar with a 2px `ink-strong` top rule: the worklist name, its source link, "as of 14:02", the position words, the current item as a typed link with its short context, **Previous (Shift+F8)**, **Next (F8)**, **Handled** (`aria-pressed`) and **Stop**.
  - Below 700px, and at 320×256, the NavigationBar shows one chip button, "Worklist 4 of 12", that opens the stepper as a bottom sheet.
  - The stepper's rendered height is added to the `sticky-offset` scroll padding, so it never hides the focused row (WCAG 2.4.11).
- **The F8 target is always named**: "F8: Worklist ‹name› 4 of 12" in the stepper or the status line. With no active worklist, F8 does nothing but say "No active worklist" and name the collect paths (Collect into worklist, `w`; Step problems on the Problems tab). There is no implicit default.
- **Stepping** moves the one primary selection and focus to the target row, like Go, and pushes one history stop; the workspace, views, split, held panes, dock and layout stay as they are. Pressed from the stepper toolbar, focus stays on the stepper. F8 does nothing while an inline editor is open or the ActionStrip holds unsent edited fields ("Finish or cancel the edit first (Esc)").
- **Program boundaries.** When the next item is on another program, the stepper names it first ("Next: build-c · clears 4 checked rows on build-b"), and F8 stops once at that boundary with one polite message; a second F8 continues. The checked set is never cleared silently (Linked views, History).
- Items decided elsewhere (for example moved to verification by another view) stay in the list, marked "decided elsewhere · skipped by F8". At either end F8 and Shift+F8 stop and say so ("End of worklist · 12 of 12 · 9 visited. F8 stops here and never wraps."). Nothing wraps or reorders silently.
- Rows are 28px, 44px below 700px and with a coarse pointer; the item grid's Handled column is a 24px checkbox (44px below 700px).
- Both surfaces read and write the same workspace records. The static 8080 surface may render the stepper in the status line; it keeps F8, the position words and the stop at the ends.

**Linking**

- **Publishes:** the primary location when a mark, bookmark, item or the stepper goes (Enter, F8, Shift+F8; one history stop); `wl=` for the active worklist (replaceState).
- **Follows:** nothing. Marks, bookmarks and worklists are independent of the selection; the current item follows the stepper, not the other way round. Selecting an entity that is in the active worklist moves the position to it and says so. Not holdable.
- **Targets rendered:** symbol and address → the Code Browser at that build · logical id → Compare · counterpart (entity @ build, with its edge word) · program bookmark address → the Code Browser at that build · each worklist's source → the link that reopens that exact result set (review queue page and match-id cursor, Problems filter, reference filter, readiness cells).
- **Peek source:** Alt+↓ on a mark or item reads its cached Peek (listing lines, facts, locator); on a program bookmark, the cached listing at its address. Nothing is fetched.
- **Absent copy:** "no longer resolves on build-d · mark kept, nothing guessed" · "source no longer available" · "Worklist not available here" · "decided elsewhere · skipped by F8" · "No active worklist".
- **Target-size exception:** none; rows are 28px, checkboxes and stepper buttons 24px; 44px below 700px and with a coarse pointer.

**Keyboard**

- From `keymap.json`: `list.mark` m then a letter; `list.jumpMark` ' then a letter (it matches the typed character and accepts dead-key input; the go-to `'` chip and `g '` are the fallbacks); `list.worklist` w; `worklist.next` F8 and `worklist.prev` Shift+F8 from anywhere; `worklist.handled` has no default key; `leader` g then ' opens this list; Ctrl/Cmd+P then ' searches marks and program bookmarks. F8 needs Fn on many macOS keyboards; the stepper buttons and the palette do the same.
- The marks, bookmarks and worklists grids are APG Grids with one tab stop: ↑/↓ rows, ←/→ cells, Enter goes, Shift+Enter opens beside, Alt+↓ peeks, Shift+F10 opens the one link menu. On a worklist row Enter makes it active. These grids bind single-letter commands, so typeahead starts with `/`.
- The item grid is not checkable: Space and x on a row never toggle Handled. The Handled cell is a checkbox reached with →, or the stepper's Handled button. Removing a mark or a worklist is in the row menu and offers undo in the status line; it has no single key.
- The stepper is an APG Toolbar with one tab stop; ←/→ move between its controls. Esc follows the one Esc order in the Keymap section.

**Accessibility**

- The mark letter, scope and state are words: "Mark a, ResourceRef::Resolve, build-a 0041b210". Glyphs are `aria-hidden`.
- Progress is text ("position 4 of 12 · 7 visited · 3 handled"). The current item row has `aria-current="true"` and the word "current".
- Each step is announced once in the polite region ("Item 4 of 12: ResourceRef::Resolve, build-b, conflict"). Marking, moving a letter, a program boundary and reaching an end are announced once; nothing is announced per poll.
- Stepping changes the selection only on the key or button press (WCAG 3.2.1). The collect dialog is a labelled modal dialog; focus returns to the invoking result set.

**Removes switching:** J17 ("walk every member of a logical identity") goes from 6 switches to 1: collect the members once, then F8, credited to U179. J36 goes from 3 to 1 through the read-only Bookmarks navigator scope and typed links, credited to U171. J22 is credited to U184.
